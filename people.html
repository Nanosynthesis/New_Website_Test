Principal Investigator

Prof. Hongyu Chen

Prof. Chen joined Westlake University in 2021 as a tenured professor in the School of Science. His group develops synthetic methodologies for complex nanostructures and studies the mechanisms that govern their formation.

  • 2021-presentProfessor, School of Science, Westlake University
  • 2016-2021Professor, Institute of Advanced Chemical Manufacturing, Nanjing Tech University
  • 2011-2016Associate Professor, Nanyang Technological University
  • 1998-2004Ph.D., Yale University
